Piotr Szefer — attorney-at-law

Commercial agreements. Intellectual property and new technologies.

I specialise in negotiating and drafting commercial agreements and provide comprehensive legal support to companies. Intellectual property and new technologies are a particular focus of my practice.

I combine experience as a lawyer, manager and entrepreneur. This allows me to approach legal matters from the perspective of those who make decisions and are accountable for their consequences.

Business perspective

I understand the responsibility that comes with decisions

Running my own business and serving on corporate bodies give me direct experience of the consequences of decisions: their costs, impact on people, working arrangements and relationships with partners. I bring this perspective to my legal advice.

I also see an agreement as the framework for future cooperation. What matters is a clear division of responsibilities, the parties’ liability and how to proceed when a project does not go to plan.

My experience in management, institutions and sport has taught me to consider the conditions in which a legal solution must work. Advice needs to be clear, practical to implement and supported by cooperation between the people responsible for putting decisions into effect.

In my advisory work, I combine this perspective with compliance, corporate governance and risk management. I focus on regulatory compliance, a transparent allocation of authority and procedures that can be applied in everyday work. Clear rules help organisations make informed decisions and support their development.
