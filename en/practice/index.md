# Legal practice

I specialise in negotiating and drafting commercial agreements. I provide comprehensive, ongoing legal support to companies, with a particular focus on intellectual property and new technologies. I also advise on sports law. My experience also covers corporate law and higher education law.

## Negotiations and commercial agreements

I draft and negotiate agreements for businesses. I consider the purpose of cooperation, the parties’ interests and how obligations will be performed. I focus on a clear division of responsibilities, liability and arrangements for changes in circumstances or disputes.

My business and management experience helps me assess how the agreed terms will work in everyday cooperation.

## Intellectual property and new technologies

I advise on acquiring rights, licensing and the use of intellectual property. I provide legal services to technology companies, including agreements with creators, rights to software, and the implementation and maintenance of IT systems.

My intellectual property practice also extends to film production, providing comprehensive support for productions in relation to commercial agreements and the protection of intellectual property.

## Ongoing legal support and corporate law

I provide comprehensive legal support to companies on their day-to-day operations, agreements and corporate bodies. I advise on compliance and corporate governance, support the development of internal regulations and help clarify decision-making rules.

I combine legal expertise with experience as a supervisory board member and as an owner overseeing my own business.

## Sports law

I advise athletes, clubs and sports organisations. My advice draws on many years of experience working in sport in Poland and internationally, including in management roles.

I have drafted, implemented and applied the internal regulations of sports organisations. I have a thorough, practical knowledge of football law.

## Higher education law

My experience includes work in the administration of Jagiellonian University and legal support to the Rector of the Medical University of Warsaw. I handled administrative proceedings, drafted internal university regulations and worked on procedures for electing academic authorities.
