Piotr Szefer — experience
and professional background

Piotr Szefer is a Polish attorney-at-law (radca prawny) specialising in negotiating and drafting commercial agreements, with a particular focus on intellectual property and new technologies. He provides comprehensive legal support to companies.

He combines legal practice with experience in management, supervisory roles and running his own business. His perspective has also been shaped by work for universities, involvement in sports organisations and international football events. Negotiation, mediation and communication are central to his professional approach.

He is a graduate of Jagiellonian University, Université de Lausanne and SGH Warsaw School of Economics. He is fluent in English.

01

Law and an understanding of business

Piotr Szefer has run his own legal practice since 2018. His work covers negotiating and drafting commercial agreements, intellectual property and technology law, and ongoing legal support for companies.

He serves on supervisory boards of companies and exercises ownership oversight of his own rapidly growing business. This experience enables him to assess legal issues in the context of financing, business organisation, relationships with partners and accountability for decisions.

His advisory work also covers compliance and corporate governance. He connects regulatory compliance with the organisation of processes, allocation of authority and risk management. His experience in drafting internal regulations helps him consider how they will be applied in an organisation’s everyday operations.

02

Management positions

From 2023 to 2025, he served as Director for Cooperation with Ekstraklasa at PKO Bank Polski. He was responsible for cooperation under the league’s title partnership, relationships with partners, negotiations and using the sponsorship project to support the bank’s business objectives.

From 2021 to 2023, he was Director of the Management Board Office at the Polish Football Association (PZPN). He led the Office, oversaw the organisation of the Management Board’s work and cooperation with stakeholders, and contributed to developing and implementing the Association’s procedures.

His management roles gave him first-hand insight into the importance of an effective flow of information, a clear allocation of authority and coordinated action. In his legal advice, this translates into attention to how an organisation makes and implements decisions.

03

Project management

He has experience in managing projects that require cooperation between teams, partners and different parts of an organisation. He gained this experience through international sports projects, among other activities.

He supplemented his practical project experience with Scrum Master training and PRINCE2 Foundation and Scrum Master Certified qualifications. This background helps him account for schedules, the allocation of tasks, dependencies between participants and project risks.

04

Universities and higher education law

Piotr Szefer’s experience in higher education law includes work for Jagiellonian University and the Medical University of Warsaw.

In the administration of Jagiellonian University, he handled administrative proceedings and drafted internal regulations. At the Medical University of Warsaw, he provided legal support to the Rector, prepared internal regulations and supported the election process for the university authorities.

These roles showed him the importance of precise procedures, a proper allocation of authority and transparent decision-making.

In 2024, he also served as Ombudsman for Academic Rights and Values at the Medical University of Warsaw. His work included mediation, supporting conflict resolution and making recommendations on the university’s operations.

05

Sport: from regional organisations to international tournaments

Piotr Szefer’s football experience spans organisational management, oversight of match organisation and safety, communication and the delivery of international events.

He served as Vice-President of the Małopolska Football Association (MZPN). From 2016 to 2021, he was a PZPN match delegate at national level, assessing match organisation and safety. He also worked on event organisation and safety at PZPN before becoming Director of its Management Board Office.

During UEFA EURO U-21 in Poland in 2017, he coordinated the match ceremonies project. At the FIFA U-20 World Cup in Poland in 2019, he was responsible for stadium communication and match ceremonies. These projects involved teams at six stadiums and cooperation with Polish and international partners.

Working on sporting events required coordinating many people, communicating precisely and responding to situations that could not be fully anticipated. This experience strengthens his awareness of organisational risks and the practical consequences of decisions. International cooperation broadened his perspective and gave him insight into the working standards of the world’s largest sports organisations.

06

Negotiation, mediation and communication

Negotiation, mediation and communication connect Piotr Szefer’s legal and management experience. Working on agreements requires recognising the parties’ interests, expressing a position precisely and establishing the terms of cooperation.

He was a stadium announcer at KS Cracovia matches from 2012 to 2016 and for the Polish national team from 2013 to 2021, including during UEFA EURO 2016 in France.

He co-developed PZPN’s training package for stadium announcers and has delivered several dozen certification courses for announcers, combining experience in front of stadium audiences with training work.

In 2012, he won the 16th ELSA National Public Speaking Competition. In 2016, he took first place in the 3rd Public Speaking Competition for Trainee Attorneys-at-Law, organised by the Regional Chamber of Attorneys-at-Law in Kraków.

In 2023, he completed a mediator certification training course organised by the Foundation of the Regional Chamber of Attorneys-at-Law in Kraków. His mediation training and experience as an ombudsman complement his negotiation skills with an understanding of conflict and ways to reach agreement.
